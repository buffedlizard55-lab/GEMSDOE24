## 1. Forensic Synthesis of Priority Repositories (`GEMSDOE24`–`GEMSDOE36`) Prior to Hypothesis Design

Evidence classes follow repository convention: **COMPUTED-ARTIFACT-FORENSICS** (exact byte/pixel checks on local and pinned GitHub artifacts), **UNVERIFIED-OWNER-REPORT** (scores pasted in the prompt without an artifact-linked DrivenData submission-page receipt), and **MECHANISTIC-ALGEBRA** (exact consequences of the official DTI formula).

1. **Why `GEMSDOE32` (`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros`, owner-reported `0.2778`) achieved the highest reported score among the 9 reviewed repositories:**
   - **Genealogy (COMPUTED-ARTIFACT-FORENSICS):** Pixel-by-pixel verification (`evidence/priority_review_20261025.json`) proves that the highest-reported submissions across `GEMSDOE24` (`0.2477`), `GEMSDOE25`/`30` (`0.2600`), `GEMSDOE28`/`31` (`0.2708`/`0.2710`), `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`), and `GEMSDOE36` (`0.2750`) all descend from **one underlying detector**: `19GEMSDOE` `H19-5` (`121,131` off-catalogue positive pixels, owner-reported `0.1922`), which fused four out-of-fold physical lines (fault-length power-law deficit at tips/step-overs, INGENIOUS springs/wells/2m-probe/paleo-sinter thermal conduits, 3DEP/LiDAR openness & scarp breaklines, and GeoDAWN gravity/magnetic horizontal-gradient worms).
   - **Step 1 — Kernel-aware Poisson-disk dot thinning (`0.1922 -> 0.2600`):** Under the official Distance-Weighted Tversky Index ($\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, triangular kernel $k(r) = \max(0, 1 - r / 300\text{ m})$), true-positive credit at a hidden fault pixel $g$ uses a **pointwise maximum** $\max_{p \in P} k(\|g - p\|)$, whereas false-positive penalty sums **additively** over every predicted off-catalogue pixel $p$. Replacing `H19-5`'s `121,131` contiguous ridge pixels with `44,090` dots spaced at $d = 2.8\text{ px}$ (`280 m`) cut false-positive mass by ~63.6% while retaining ~78% of along-ridge kernel credit.
   - **Step 2 — Catalogue mapping-apron collar clearance (`0.2600 -> 0.2708 -> 0.2778`):** `GEMSDOE31`/`28` (`H27-4`, `40,199` dots) deleted `3,891` dots within $d \le 1.0\text{ px}$ (`100 m`) of `existing_faults.tif`, and `GEMSDOE32` (`H33-2-B2`, `37,654` dots) deleted all `6,436` dots within $d \le 2.0\text{ px}$ (`200 m`) of `existing_faults.tif` (`0` dots added). Because 1:250,000 Qfaults traces have up to ~400 m positional offset relative to 1 m LiDAR scarps (Hermant et al., 2025), off-catalogue predictions within 100–200 m of digitized traces frequently fire on the scarp apron of the *already-mapped* fault rather than a distinct unmapped fault. Deleting predictions with lost TP credit $L$ and saved FP mass $M$ increases DTI whenever $L / M < \alpha D / [1 - (1 - \beta) D] \approx 0.055$ at $D \approx 0.26$.
   - **Hard ceiling of post-processing (`0.2778` vs `0.3195` / `0.4413`):** Subset pruning of `H19-5` cannot recover a single unmapped fault missed by `H19-5`, and any further subset or minor perturbation of `H19-5`/`D2.8`/`H33-2-B2` has $>95\%$ 3-px dot overlap with existing registry rasters (strictly prohibited by Parallel-Run Protocol Rule 1 and the zero-copy mandate). Surpassing that ceiling legitimately requires a genuinely independent multi-physics + geothermal-upflow OOF detector paired with 300 m kernel-optimal allocation, benchmarked against a reconstructed out-of-fold baseline under `SegmentHoldout`.

### 3.2 Exact Engineered Feature Specification (`18` Static Label-Free Features)

7. `gvc_cond_clay_grad`: Horizontal gradient magnitude $\|\nabla \log(1 + \max(0, \text{cond\_surf}))\|_2$ (`MT` smectite clay-cap lateral boundary).
8. `gvc_tmi_tilt`: Miller & Singh (1994) magnetic tilt angle $\arctan(\text{tmi\_vg} / \sqrt{\text{tmi\_hg}^2 + 10^{-6}})$, plus `gvc_tmi_tilt_grad` $= \|\nabla \theta_{\text{TMI}}\|_2$ (`#9`, peaks directly over vertical magnetic susceptibility contacts independent of depth).
9. *(see #8/#9 above)*
10. `gvc_grav_tilt_grad`: Horizontal gradient magnitude $\|\nabla \arctan(\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_vg} / \sqrt{\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_hg}^2 + 10^{-6}})\|_2$ (depth-normalized basement density-contact sharpness).
11. `gvc_tmi_shallow_demag`: Standardized shallow vertical magnetic difference $G_{\sigma=20}(z_{\text{TMI\_up150}} - z_{\text{tmi\_rtp}}) - (z_{\text{TMI\_up150}} - z_{\text{tmi\_rtp}})$ capturing short-wavelength near-surface demagnetization lows relative to `150 m` upward-continued TMI.

### 3.3 Reconstructed Baselines, Candidate Arms & Submodular 300 m Kernel Allocator
- **Training sample construction per fold (strictly inside `fold["train"]` using `fold["visible"]` ONLY):**
  - Positives: all pixels where `fold["train"] & fold["visible"]` is `True`.
  - Negatives: deterministic random sample (`max_neg = 220,000`, `seed = 20261025 + fold_id`) from `fold["train"] & (EDT(~fold["visible"]) >= 9.0)`.
  - Inner spatial calibration split: `20 km` (`200 px`) spatial blocks of the training sample (`block_id % 6 == 0` held out strictly inside `fold["train"]` for monotone `IsotonicRegression(y_min=0.0, y_max=1.0, out_of_bounds="clip")` probability calibration; remaining `5/6` blocks fit a 2-member `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` ensemble with `(max_iter=220, learning_rate=0.05, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=80, l2_regularization=3.0)` at seeds `100 + fold_id` and `200 + fold_id`).
- **Exact Minoux Lazy-Greedy 300 m Triangular-Kernel Submodular Dot Allocator (`submodular_dti_allocate`):**
  - Given calibrated probability map $\hat{p}(x) \in [0, 1]$ on eligible off-catalogue pixels (`valid & (EDT(~visible) > 2.0)`), defines monotone submodular expected weighted-TP coverage $U(S) = \sum_y \hat{p}(y) \max_{s \in S} k(\|y - s\|_2)$ over the exact 29-offset $R = 3.0\text{ px}$ triangular kernel, subject to minimum inter-dot spacing $d_{\min} = 2.5\text{ px}$ (`250 m`) and budget $K = \text{round}(37654 \cdot |\text{eval\_offcat}| / |\text{footprint\_offcat}|)$ (`37,654` dots on the full footprint; by Dinkelbach's theorem for linear-fractional Tversky ratios, selected dots take value `1.0` and unselected pixels `0.0` in `float32`).
- **Four evaluated series (`2` baselines + `2` candidate experiments $\le 3$ budget):**
  - **`B0` (`OOF-H24-Ridge`)**: Reconstructed legacy 30-feature single-HGB + `ridge_nms` (`0.0245` budget) from `h24_2_experiment.py` on `SegmentHoldout` with leak-free `fold["visible"]` negative sampling.
  - **`B1` (`OOF-Base-Submod`)**: Reconstructed 30-feature calibrated 2-HGB baseline + `submodular_dti_allocate` (`37,654`-dot matched budget).
  - **`E1` (`H24-GVC1`, Experiment 1/3)**: 48-feature (`30` base + `18` `GVC1`) calibrated 2-HGB + `submodular_dti_allocate` (`37,654`-dot matched budget).
  - **`E2` (`H24-GVC1-Resid`, Experiment 2/3)**: Identical to `E1` with training-only `NuisanceResidualizer(distance_columns=2, alpha=100.0)` fit strictly on `fold["train"]` (`road_m`, `claim_m`, `block_1..4`) applied to continuous features prior to HGB fitting.
