## 3. Fixed Specification of the Selected Experiment (`scripts/run_fuse_lane.py`)

We use our session budget (3 experiments maximum, <2 hours) to execute the three preregistered arms of **Rank 1 (`H24-FUSE`)** in a single reproducible script `scripts/run_fuse_lane.py` without post-hoc parameter tuning:

1. **Data & Support Verification:**
   - Verify SHA-256 checksums of `data/bridge/sample_submission.tif`, `data/bridge/labels.tif`, `data/prepared/features.npy`, `data/external/lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif`, `data/external/geodawn_rad_u8.tif`, and `data/external/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif`.
   - Compute `SAHG1` surfaces (`G_strike`, `G_sat`, `R_alt`, `B_step`, `H_conduit`) via `gems.sahg1.build_surfaces(...)` and verify exact $9\times 9$ discrete receptive-field support via `gems.sahg1.verify_receptive_field_support(support, raw_valid, radius_px=4)`.
   - Extract the 4 engineered channels onto the $5,167,373$ footprint pixels (`X_eng`, float32) and free the raw 2D band arrays immediately so peak RSS stays below $1.8\,\text{GB}$.

2. **Leakage Canary (Gate 4):**
   - Run `context.canary` on all 4 engineered channels (`G_sat`, `R_alt`, `B_step`, `H_conduit`), `G_strike`, and the base channels under `SegmentHoldout` (`gems-segment-pooled-v1`). Halt immediately if any feature has pooled $\text{AUC} > 0.90$ or $< 0.10$.

3. **Three Preregistered OOF Arms (`SegmentHoldout`, `buffer_px=15`, `SEED=20261025`):**
   - For each fold $f \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ (`NW`, `NE_LidarGapHeavy`, `SW`, `SE`):
     - Compute `dist_visible = distance_transform_edt(~fold["visible"])` using **only** `fold["visible"]` (never touching `fold["truth"]` or hidden components).
     - Positive training pool: `fold["train"] & fold["visible"]` (sample up to `20,000` pixels with `rng = np.random.default_rng(SEED + f)`).
     - Negative training pool: `fold["train"] & ~fold["visible"] & (dist_visible >= 9)` (sample up to `60,000` pixels).
     - **Experiment Arm 1 (`H24-FUSE-RAW`):** Fit `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=80, max_leaf_nodes=15, learning_rate=0.08, l2_regularization=5.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=SEED)` on the 31 raw features (`27` base + `G_sat`, `R_alt`, `B_step`, `H_conduit`). Predict OOF probabilities on `fold["valid"]` in chunks of `75,000` pixels. Emit per-fold dots via `priority_poisson(p_raw, support & fold["valid"] & ~fold["visible"], budget, min_dist=3)` where `budget = round(0.0073 * fold["valid"].sum())`.
     - **Experiment Arm 2 (`H24-FUSE-RESID` — Documented Next Step #4):** Fit `NuisanceResidualizer(distance_columns=2, alpha=100.0)` on a uniform `30,000`-pixel sample drawn strictly from `fold["train"]` using the strict nuisance matrix `C = [log1p(road_m), log1p(claim_m), block_1, block_2, block_3, block_4]`. Transform the 31 geological features via `resid.transform(C, X_31, remove=True)` on `train_sel` and on `fold["valid"]` chunks, fit the identical `HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, predict OOF probabilities `p_resid`, and emit per-fold dots with the identical budget and `min_dist=3`.
     - **Experiment Arm 3 (`H24-FUSE-HCW` — Hydrothermal-Conduit-Weighted OOF-HGB):** Combine the fold-local residualized OOF probability `p_resid` with the physical hydrothermal-basement conduit factor `H_conduit` and saturating scarp gate `G_sat`:
       $$S_{\text{hcw}}(x) = p_{\text{resid}}(x) \cdot \bigl(0.25 + 0.75\,H_{\text{conduit}}(x)\bigr) \cdot \bigl(0.30 + 0.70\,G_{\text{sat}}(x)\bigr)$$
       and emit per-fold dots via `priority_poisson(S_hcw, support & fold["valid"] & ~fold["visible"], budget, min_dist=3)`. This tests whether weighting the learned OOF probability by hydrothermal/basement corroboration decorrelates the emitted dot geometry below the $0.70$ competing-method-lane overlap threshold while preserving learned multi-physics skill.

4. **Evaluation, Novelty, C2ST & Packaging:**
   - Score all three arms with `context.summarize(..., ci_draws=1999, seed=SEED)` and compute paired block-bootstrap $\Delta\text{DTI}$ and 95% CIs against `oof_multiphysics_hgb_d3` (`0.13000`) and `H24-SAHG1` (`0.09672`).
   - Check pre-placement surface and final-dot novelty against all 46 priority rasters (`registry/priority_rasters.json`), prior 2026-10-25 session rasters, and all 84 peer blobs (`registry/peer_rasters_20261025.json`) via `compare_prospective_registry`.
   - Run the exact-file C2ST nuisance audit (`gems.c2s2.c2s2_test`, 10 km block swaps, Holm adjustment) on the emitted candidate.
   - Package the primary unique GeoTIFF pair (`-zeros.tif` with all-finite `[0, 1]` values and `-nan.tif` twin) for the best method-lane-unique arm (`H24-FUSE-HCW` if `FUSE-RAW`/`FUSE-RESID` exceed `0.70` method-lane overlap, or the highest-HOLDOUT-DTI arm passing prospective novelty), validate with `submission.check_variants` and `preflight_submission.py`, and record the complete JSON run card in `evidence/fuse_run_card_20261025.json` and `docs/data/fuse_run_card.json`.
   - Promote (`verdict: "promote"`, `ok_to_submit: true`) **if and only if** all gates pass simultaneously (beats `0.13000` OOF baseline, canary PASS, novelty PASS including literal rule, C2ST PASS, format PASS, and selector authorization). Otherwise emit an unmistakable `verdict: "negative"` (`DOWNLOAD: YES · SUBMIT: NO`).
