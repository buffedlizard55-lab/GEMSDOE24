### Interpretation at the level the evidence supports

The hypothesis was that all-channel nuisance correction discarded useful
tectonic morphology. Preserving those channels did **not** produce a detectable
paired gain in this fixed test. Failure to detect a difference is not proof the
models are equivalent, nor proof that all selective interventions fail. It is a
reason **not to promote this configuration**.

C2ST detects association, not the causal origin of a signal. Terrain, access and
mining all can covary with genuine geology. Conversely, raw catalogue-trained
models can converge on mapped range fronts without discovering concealed faults.
A good discrimination score alone does not identify which process occurred.

The candidate uses the frozen complete 9x9 SAHG1 support mask, covering only
**54.7586%** of the footprint. Public labels outside it still contribute missed
truth. It is not a full-coverage solution; it does not inherit RSHG1's coverage
claim. Physical-feature preprocessing contains survey-wide label-free statistics,
so only the **new nuisance fit** is claimed training-only. Model scores are
case-control-trained ranking signals, not calibrated posterior probabilities.
Final emitted values are binary `{0,1}`.

## Why H33-B2 reportedly scored highest

Its **0.2778 is OWNER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED**, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The brief's
0.3195 and 0.4413 leader figures conflict; neither is a verified current leader.
No artifact-linked submission-page receipt was retrieved or manufactured.

Frozen geometry and source reconstruction show H33-B2 is the predecessor's
40,199-dot emission with 2,545 catalogue-near dots removed, leaving 37,654 and no
added dots. Relative to the 44,090-dot ancestor, it removes 6,436. This supports
an emission-efficiency hypothesis, not a new detector or demonstrated vent
localization. The old rasters were inspected for learning/comparison only.

For pooled DTI with truth mass N, weighted truth credit T and FP mass F,

`D = T / (0.2T + 0.2F + 0.8N)`.

Deleting predictions that lose truth credit L and save FP mass S improves D only
when **`L(0.2F + 0.8N) < 0.2TS`** (positive denominators). This is algebra, not an
observed hidden confusion matrix. A catalogue-proximal removed dot could carry
both FP mass and useful hidden-fault credit. Neither L nor S is available here.
We cannot attribute all the reported gain to FP reduction, establish an optimal
dot budget, or forecast a new score above 0.2778 from catalogue recovery.

**Can a new method score higher?** It is scientifically possible, but this test
does not establish one. The useful falsification result is that selective
nuisance removal alone did not add measured paired recovery and still converged
on existing method lanes. Substantive new localization evidence—not another
cosmetic thinning of an old winner—is the appropriate research direction.
