### Physical/metric interpretation — inference, not proof

Thinning redundant emissions can preserve substantial 300 m triangular-kernel
credit while reducing false-positive mass. Catalogue-flank removal may save
additional FP mass when those dots have little hidden-fault credit. But adjacent
pixels are not automatically false positives: concealed extensions or differently
mapped traces may be close to a public fault. Neither hidden TP loss nor hidden
FP saving is observed here.

For `D = T / [(1−β)T + αF + βG]`, a deletion losing weighted TP credit `L` and
saving FP mass `M` helps **iff**

`L < α D M / [1 − (1−β)D]`.

This is algebra under a fixed hidden truth count G, not a score forecast. It
explains an efficiency mechanism that *could* help; it does not establish why an
organizer score changed. H33-B2 adds no new coordinates relative to that base,
so its geometry is not evidence of a new hydrothermal discovery mechanism.

## Four hypotheses, before implementation

[Preregistration](09_preregistration_magnetic_trough_2026-10-25.md) was committed
as `3589290`; the support/rank-check amendment and implementation were committed
as `b6b4cc0` **before any measured outcome**. Expected benefit was qualitative,
not a fabricated numerical score projection.

1. **MTR1 — tested:** RTP two-shoulder signed magnetic trough with ±300 m
   cross-normal shoulders, four normals, tangent persistence ±400 m; local
   contractor Th/K depletion. Hypothesis: a concealed alteration corridor
   could lack a surface scarp. Difference: two-sided trough geometry rather
   than magnetic contact edges, terrain ridges or catalogue pruning. Highest
   testability/cost ranking; uncertain benefit; low CPU cost.
2. **MTR2 — not run:** the same RTP trough plus cached TMI and extension band 4
   `TMI_up150`, requiring trough survival under upward continuation. Intended
   to distinguish deeper corridors from cultural short wavelengths, not an
   independent sensor. Moderate uncertainty, medium implementation cost.
3. **MTR3 — not run:** RTP with extension bands 1 `ThK` and 3 `UTh`, testing
   compositional asymmetry across both shoulders rather than scalar fusion.
   Lower confidence; low implementation cost; quantization can erase contrast.
4. **MTR4 — not run:** RTP with cached `cond_surf`, testing a conductivity ridge
   centred between both magnetic shoulders, not merely a contact/clay-cap edge.
   Low expected localization leverage, low implementation cost.

No idea required a newly obtained external source. Cached channel names, bands,
quantization and grid metadata were actually inspected. This does not imply a new
external-data idea is viable without checking its official source.

## Fixed operator, measured holdout and limits

`M = 1−exp(−paired_trough)`; `R = 1−exp(−local_ThK_depletion)`;
`candidate = M*(0.5+0.5*R)`. Scales, weights, spacing and budget were not selected
on holdout. There is no supervised model or nuisance/scaling fit in this formula;
no catalogue-derived detector feature, prior prediction or terrain channel.

**HOLDOUT-DTI**, evaluator **`gems-segment-pooled-v1`**, **60,988 withheld positive
pixels / 3,199 raster connected components**, alpha .2, beta .8, 300 m triangular
kernel; pooled TPw/FPw/FNw, not an average of fold ratios:

| Fixed arm | HOLDOUT-DTI | Approximate 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| Paired RTP magnetic trough only | 0.06166451764562432 | [0.05571278278087662, 0.06827329304394117] |
| MTR1, with local Th/K | 0.06441156357414125 | [0.058299401413499985, 0.07112431360797855] |

CIs use 1,999 resamples of 583 active 10 km blocks. They are conditional internal
validation intervals, not uncertainty about private faults or leaderboard scores.
Structures may cross bootstrap blocks. The point difference is not a certified
current-best win; no paired difference CI was computed. No chance-control
experiment was run, so a nonzero DTI is not proof of skill above chance.

Whole 8-connected components touching a validation quadrant or its 1.5 km collar
are hidden globally; truth is scored once inside its quadrant; visible catalogue
pixels are excluded exactly. Raster connectivity approximates expert segment IDs.
The label raster is a public/mirrored catalogue surrogate, not private new-fault
truth. The masked, full-grid shared metric is reused.

Every actual source/nontrivial factor and support mask had a feature-alone leakage
canary, both polarities: RTP .50779, ThK .48849, support .49005, trough .49811,
ratio depletion .49559 pooled **diagnostic AUC**. None exceeded the .90 rule;
near-chance values do not prove absence of all leakage or hydrothermal specificity.
The candidate is a fixed combination of those factors, not an extra fitted input.

**Named non-fault mimic:** magnetite-poor alluvial palaeochannel with K-rich
detritus. Weathering, remanence, lithologic strips and acquisition effects remain
alternatives. No retrieved paper or in-situ measurement identifies alteration,
permeability, temperature or a reservoir at these predicted locations.

Other limitations found during review:
- The frozen upstream ratio encoding used survey-wide 1st/99th percentiles.
  It is reused as a fixed physical-data product, not fitted nuisance removal;
  it is nevertheless transductive preprocessing, not a prospective training-only
  reconstruction from raw channels. No claim of independent original-file
  authentication or absolute Th/K concentration follows from byte ranks.
- The pre-run 72-pixel Euclidean support guard reduces edge effects but is **not a
  formal zero-influence proof for the corners of finite square Gaussian support**.
  A future newly preregistered method should use a footprint validity mask covering
  its complete discrete receptive field. This frozen negative raster is not
  modified after its stop, and no edge-ablation experiment is launched.
- The exact-file nuisance audit and comparable current-best OOF reconstruction
  were not completed after the duplicate stop. Neither is quietly marked passed.

## Novelty incompatibility — failure retained

Exact full-footprint raw Spearman checks were performed before placement and on
final dots; additional triangular-kernel sensitivity used a fixed 200,000-pixel
sample. Maximum measured absolute rank across those diagnostics: **0.0424155764**
pre-placement, **0.0775609054** final. Exact all-dot directional overlap at radius
3 is **1.0** against `registry-039`, the spacing-5 calibration lattice.

The lattice covers **99.87132726822701%** of the footprint and **100%** of the
supported candidate domain at that radius. Thus the literal overlap rule is
non-discriminating on this domain. This explains the veto but does not authorize
ignoring it. The record remains **duplicate / negative / not eligible**.
