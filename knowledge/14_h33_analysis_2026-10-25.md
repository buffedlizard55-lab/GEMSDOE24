## 2. Competition metric mechanics (verified source)

Official reference: [DrivenData problem page §Performance Metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric). Reference solution: [drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution) (training Tversky `α=0.2, β=0.8`).

Evaluation DTI (distance-weighted Tversky):
```
K(d) = max(0, 1 − d/300m),  R=3 px at 100 m
DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + α·FP_w + β·FN_w),  α=0.2, β=0.8
```
This is mathematically `TP_w / (0.2·TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·|G|)` where `|G|` is hidden-truth pixel mass. With sparse hidden truth (`|G|` ≈0.24% of footprint, per `evidence/evidence` spacing-5 lattice calibration), the `0.2·FP_w` term dominates denominator for dense emitters (121k ridge pixels). Halving `FP_w` can double DTI even with unchanged `TP_w`.

Verified evaluator in this repo: `src/gems/metric.py` (`dti_components_exact`, `dti_score_fast`) with triangular kernel `R=3 px`, pooled `TP_w/FP_w/FN_w` aggregation. Cross-checked against organizer worked example (`verify_organizer_worked_example`).

## 3. Why thinning wins — three physical/statistical mechanisms

### 3.1 Triangular-kernel packing
A 1-px contiguous ridge and a 2.8-px Poisson-disk dotted set have very different `FP_w` but similar `TP_w` under a 300 m kernel. Each dot's kernel covers neighbors with weight 0.67 at 100 m, 0.33 at 200 m, 0 at 300 m. Spacing dots at 280 m eliminates overlapping kernels' redundant FP mass while preserving TP credit. Empirically: H19-5→D2.8 reduces pixels 121k→44k (−64%) while `TP_w` decays sublinearly; DTI rises 0.1922→0.2600 in owner-reported numbers.

### 3.2 Catalogue-flank penalty
Surfaces trained on the public USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue (60,988 pixels, `data/bridge/labels.tif`) peak on scarp flanks due to 100 m DEM/magnetic smoothing. When evaluator masks `d_catalogue=0` (visible known faults), dots at `1≤d≤2 px` (100–200 m from known trace) incur full `FP_w` unless an unmapped fault lies within 300 m of the known trace. Deleting 6,436 flank dots (D2.8→H33-B2) satisfies improvement condition `ΔTP_w/ΔFP_w < α·DTI/(1−(1−β)DTI)` for DTI≈0.26. H33-B2 has **0 dots within 200 m of catalogue** (`evidence/peer_forensics_20261025.json`).

### 3.3 Budget-density matching to hidden truth
Public catalogue density ≈1.18% of footprint (60,988/5,167,373). Hidden truth is ≈5× sparser (≈0.24%), calibrated from spacing-5 lattice and holdout thinning (`Holdout` with `keep_frac=0.20`). A 37,654-dot budget (≈0.73% of footprint, ≈3× hidden density) matches Tversky's optimal precision-recall trade-off under `β=0.8`. Denser emissions (121k) over-predict; sparser (37k) better balances `β·FN_w` vs. `α·FP_w`.

## 4. Why this plateaus — and how to genuinely beat 0.2778

H33-B2 **adds zero new geological detections** — it is pure post-processing of H19-5. Its DTI gain is entirely `FP_w` reduction on already-mapped flank loci, not new TP on unmapped intra-basin/piedmont faults.

To exceed 0.2778 with ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED significance, a method must:

1. **Generate new TP** on faults invisible at 1:250k airphoto / INGENIOUS scale: decimetre-scale piedmont scarps under Quaternary fan cover, or blind geothermal conduits with no surface scarp. This requires **strike-coherent LiDAR geometry** (structure-tensor `θ` with along-strike persistence) that rejects contour-parallel Lake Lahontan shoreline terraces — the dominant false positive in NW Nevada (see `knowledge/03_verified_sources_2026-10-02.md` USGS 3DEP, GeoDAWN report).

2. **Corroborate with subsurface/hydrothermal physics**: gravity/magnetic horizontal gradient steps (`iso_grav_anom_hg`, `tmi_hg`), basement-depth inflection (`depth_to_base_surf` gradient), and Th-immobile K/U alteration halos (`K−Th` + `1−ThK` local contrast at stable `Th`, `UTh` ratio) — signals that persist where LiDAR scarps are buried. SAHG1's saturating gate `G_sat = G_strike/(G_strike+0.015)` implements this: any decimetre scarp above noise is equally eligible; ranking is then by `√(R_alt·B_step)·√B_step`.

3. **Pass holdout before spending a slot**: H33-B2's dense-catalogue holdout is non-inferior (old regime ~5× denser than hidden). The required test is **pooled HOLDOUT-DTI on whole-component hide-and-recover** with 1.5 km collar (`gems-segment-pooled-v1`, 60,988 withheld positives, 3,199 components, 10 km block bootstrap, 1,999 draws). Our same-protocol OOF-HGB baseline is **HOLDOUT-DTI 0.13000 [0.11565, 0.14396]** (see `evidence/sahg1_run_card_20261025.json`). SAHG1 hand-crafted product scored 0.09672 [0.08657, 0.10703] — a paired loss of −0.03328 [−0.04658, −0.02062] vs. OOF-HGB, proving emission surgery alone cannot beat learned multi-physics.

**PhD judgement:** Beating 0.2778 ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED likely requires a **fold-local supervised learner that fuses strike-projected LiDAR geometry with hydrothermal/basement corroboration and nuisance-residualized training** — i.e., the SAHG1 features inside an OOF-HGB. That is the single hypothesis worth a weekly slot, and the only one that must be validated on holdout (≥0.13000) before submission. All pure thinning/re-weighting of H19-5 has saturated.
