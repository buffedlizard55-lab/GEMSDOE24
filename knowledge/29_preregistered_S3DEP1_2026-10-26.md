   Fit `Ridge(alpha=100.0)` from `NuisanceResidualizer._basis(c_bg)` directly onto background log-odds $\ell_{\text{bg}} = \mathrm{logit}(\hat{p}_{\text{bg}})$ (**without** conditioning on slope/relief, preventing collinear valley-road survival) and subtract `(ridge.predict(basis) - mean_nuis_)` at prediction time on both Stage-1 and Stage-2 surfaces.
