## Why h33-h33-2-b2 (GEMSDOE32) got the group's highest reported score 0.2778 — PhD-level answer

**Forensics (byte-exact):** The nine priority sites form one nested family (see `registry/priority_rasters.json` + `evidence/peer_forensics_20261025.json`):
- GEMSDOE19 H19-5: 121,131 ridge-thinned dots, ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED? No — OWNER-REPORTED 0.1922.
- GEMSDOE25/30 D2.8: 44,090 dots via Poisson-disk d=2.8 px thinning of H19-5, OWNER-REPORTED 0.2600.
- GEMSDOE31 solo D2.8: 40,199 dots = D2.8 minus 3,891 dots at `d_cat ≤1 px` (100 m) from known faults, OWNER-REPORTED 0.2708.
- **GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2: 37,654 dots = solo minus 2,545 dots at `1< d_cat ≤2 px` (≤200 m), OWNER-REPORTED 0.2778** (verified construction: 6,436 dots removed from D2.8 at `d_cat ≤2 px`, zero added).
- GEMSDOE36 38854: 38,854 = 37,654 +1,200 extras, OWNER-REPORTED 0.2750 (Δ -0.0028).

**Mechanism (DTI budget economics):** DTI = TP_w / (TP_w +0.2 FP_w +0.8 FN_w). Competition truth is ~5× sparser than the mapped catalogue (≈0.24% vs ~1.2% footprint; see `docs/data/footprint.json` 5,167,373 footprint pixels). Known faults are masked exactly (staff clarification https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4), so a dot within 200 m of a known fault almost never earns TP_w (needs hidden truth within 300 m) but always pays 0.2·FP_w. Break-even credit at 0.2778 is `k >0.2·DTI =0.0556` (GEMSDOE32 derives 0.0548 from paired draws, matching theory 0.0556). `D2.8`→`B=2` satisfied `ΔTP_w/ΔFP_w <0.0556` by removing low-credit flank dots. Runner-up 38,854 adding 1,200 dots beyond B=2 pays -0.0028 because those extras are below break-even.

**Why emission surgery plateaus:** H33-B2 adds zero new detections beyond H19-5's field — it only re-spends FP mass. Beating 0.2778 needs **higher-placed mass** (higher mean TP_w per dot), not more/less of same mass. At 37,654 dots, +8% mean credit →0.30 (GEMSDOE32 audit). Public #1 `xiaofanhu 0.4413` (OWNER-REPORTED, read 2026-10-25, see brief) needs ≈+59% mean credit vs H19-5 field — impossible without genuinely better fault placement (e.g., blind intrabasin faults with no LiDAR scarp but with thermal/gravity signal).

**Can we beat 0.2778?** In-principle yes, but only by discovering unmapped fault segments with higher credit density. Our spatially-blocked **HOLDOUT-DTI** (60,988 withheld positives, whole components, 1.5 km collar) is the honest internal proxy: SAHG1 0.09672, OOF-HGB-D3 0.13000 on same protocol (both HOLDOUT-DTI, 95% CI). Neither predicts 0.2778 (different truth density). Emission policy alone (dotting/B=2) already exhausted its gain in this family; further gains require new geological features orthogonal to catalogue-scrap proximity.

## Frozen GT1 operator (no holdout tuning)

1. **Thermal anomaly field T(x):** From 2m probe shapefile, use UTM_E/N (EPSG:32611 via NAD83→32611) and F2mDAB (field F2mDAB = 2 m DAB, °C above background per README.txt in zip). For each footprint pixel, IDW power 2 over nearest ≤100 probes within 5000 m radius; weight w=d^-2, T = sum(w·F2mDAB)/sum(w). Clip to [0, 10] then normalize T_norm = clip(T,0,10)/10. Where no probe within 5 km, T_norm=0. Verified: 2782 probes inside footprint, F2mDAB distribution mean 0.72, SD ~3, 440 points >2°C, 53 >10°C — sufficient support; 5 km radius covers 94% footprint.

2. **Basement-hinge field B(x):** Using prepared `depth_to_base_surf` (NaN outside valid), compute 3×3 central-difference gradient magnitude |∇depth| (Sobel via np.gradient on gap-filled DEM trick with distance-transform nearest valid). Compute gravity HG `iso_grav_anom_hg` robust sigmoid σ(z)=(1+exp(-(z-med)/IQR))^-1 on support (med/IQR on support only). B_raw = σ(|∇depth|) *0.6 + σ(iso_grav_anom_hg)*0.4. Then B = clip(B_raw,0,1).

3. **Alluvial-flat mask F(x):** det_elev_slope in degrees (prepared). F = exp(-slope/5°) — favors flat basins (<2° → ~0.67) penalizes steep range fronts (>15° →0.05). This is veto, not primary.

4. **Support mask:** raw_valid = fp & finite(depth_to_base) & finite(iso_grav_anom_hg) & finite(det_elev_slope). Support = binary_erosion(raw_valid, 9×9) (radius 4 px =400 m) ensuring complete stencil (like SAHG1). Thermal gaps do NOT affect support (T=0 neutral).

5. **Candidate surface S:** S = B * (0.3 +0.7·T_norm) * (0.5+0.5·F) ∈[0,1] on support, 0 elsewhere. Saturating? No tuning; B is primary hinge, T thermal gate (factor 0.3–1.0), F flatness boost.

6. **Emission:** Deterministic descending S, priority Poisson min_dist=3 px (like SAHG1/MTR1), budget k=round(0.0073·|footprint|)=37722 dots. Eligible = support & ~visible catalogue (pixel-exact). No catalogue distance halo beyond visible. No zero backfill.

7. **Ablation:** B_only = B * F (without thermal) at same budget/support to measure thermal Δ.

8. **Leakage canary:** Test each raw input (depth gradient, iso_grav_anom_hg, det_slope, T_norm) plus B and S alone per fold; AUC>0.90 or <0.10 quarantine.

9. **Novelty:** Pre-placement surface Spearman exact over footprint vs 46 priority rasters + 83 peer blobs; final dots same vs same plus MTR1/SAHG1/ASPC. Thresholds 0.90 rho /0.70 overlap at ≤3 px.

10. **C2ST nuisance audit (optional limited):** Not full C2ST due to time; report descriptive road/claim AUC only as diagnostic, not gate, unless time permits.
