## 3. Exact Mathematical & Algorithmic Specification of `H24-XVAR1`

### 3.1 Label-Free Azimuthal Semivariogram & Split-Domain Channels (`8` new channels in `data/prepared/xvar1_features.dat`)

All 8 channels are computed strictly from sensor rasters **without reading `labels.tif` or `existing_faults.tif`**, asserting `FORBIDDEN_COLUMNS` (`dist_known_fault_px`, `fault_dist`, `qfault_dist`, `known_fault`) are never accessed, and preserving `NaN` outside valid sensor support:

Let robust standardized field $z(y, x) = \mathrm{clip}\bigl((f(y, x) - \mathrm{med}(f)) / \mathrm{IQR}(f),\, -8, 8\bigr)$ on valid pixels (nearest-filled strictly inside convolution stencils, masked back to `NaN` outside valid support). For strike index $m \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ ($\theta_m = m\pi/4$) with discrete unit step $(\Delta y_m, \Delta x_m)$ along $\mathbf{u}_m$ and orthogonal cross-strike normal $\mathbf{n}_m = \mathbf{u}_{(m+2)\bmod 4}$:
- Directional empirical semivariance at lag $h \in \{2, 3, 6\}$ px (`200 m`, `300 m`, `600 m`), smoothed over Gaussian window $\sigma = 3.0$ px (`300 m`):
  $$\gamma_m(x; f, h) = \frac{1}{2}\, G_{\sigma=3.0} * \bigl(z(x + h\mathbf{u}_m) - z(x)\bigr)^2, \qquad \gamma_{\text{iso}}(x; f, h) = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{m=0}^3 \gamma_m(x; f, h).$$
- Offset half-plane centers across strike at $L = 5$ px (`500 m`): $x_{\pm, m} = x \pm L\mathbf{n}_m$, signed two-sided log-semivariogram jump:
  $$J_m(x; f, h) = \log\frac{\gamma_{\text{iso}}(x_{+, m}; f, h) + \varepsilon}{\gamma_{\text{iso}}(x_{-, m}; f, h) + \varepsilon}, \qquad \varepsilon = 10^{-3}.$$

The 8 `XVAR1` channels are:
1. **`xvar_rtp_twosided_jump`:** Multi-lag persistent two-sided magnetic texture jump $\max_{m} \sqrt{\lvert J_m(x; \text{rtp}, 2)\rvert \cdot \lvert J_m(x; \text{rtp}, 6)\rvert}$ where $\mathrm{sign}(J_m(\text{rtp}, 2)) = \mathrm{sign}(J_m(\text{rtp}, 6))$.
2. **`xvar_grav_twosided_jump`:** Multi-lag persistent two-sided isostatic-gravity texture jump $\max_{m} \sqrt{\lvert J_m(x; \text{iso\_grav\_anom}, 2)\rvert \cdot \lvert J_m(x; \text{iso\_grav\_anom}, 6)\rvert}$ with consistent lag sign.
3. **`xvar_mag_grav_concordance`:** Azimuth- and polarity-locked magnetic–gravity texture step $\max_{m} \sqrt{\max\bigl(0,\, J_m(x; \text{rtp}, 2)\cdot J_m(x; \text{iso\_grav\_anom}, 2)\bigr)}$, firing where both magnetic and gravity short-wavelength roughness drop on the same hanging-wall block across strike.
4. **`xvar_spectral_roughness_step`:** Two-sided jump across strike ($L = 5$ px) in the Spector–Grant depth-proxy log-roughness ratio $\rho_{\text{SG}}(x) = \log\bigl((\gamma_{\text{iso}}(x; \text{rtp}, 2) + \varepsilon) / (\gamma_{\text{iso}}(x; \text{rtp}, 6) + \varepsilon)\bigr)$, directly sensing abrupt footwall-to-hanging-wall magnetic source-depth steps.
5. **`xvar_up150_persistence`:** Upward-continuation texture-step persistence $\max_m \sqrt{\lvert J_m(x; \text{tmi}, 3)\rvert \cdot \lvert J_m(x; \text{TMI\_up150}, 3)\rvert}$ inside `TMI_up150 > 0` valid rank support, suppressing shallow surficial clast/cultural noise that decays at `150 m` upward continuation.
6. **`xvar_anisotropy_corridor`:** Cross-strike vs along-strike directional semivariogram anisotropy $\max_m \frac{1}{2}\Bigl[\log\frac{\gamma_{\perp,m}(\text{rtp}, 3)+\varepsilon}{\gamma_{\parallel,m}(\text{rtp}, 3)+\varepsilon} + \log\frac{\gamma_{\perp,m}(\text{iso\_grav\_anom}, 3)+\varepsilon}{\gamma_{\parallel,m}(\text{iso\_grav\_anom}, 3)+\varepsilon}\Bigr]_+$, positive along elongated linear structural corridors where fields stay smooth along strike ($\parallel$) and vary sharply across strike ($\perp$).
7. **`xvar_cond_basement_step`:** Two-sided split-domain step ($L = 5$ px) combining MT surface conductivity (`cond_surf` smectite/illite clay alteration) and basin thickness (`depth_to_base_surf`): $\max_m \sqrt{\lvert z_{\sigma=2}(\text{cond\_surf})(x_{+,m}) - z_{\sigma=2}(\text{cond\_surf})(x_{-,m})\rvert \cdot \lvert z_{\sigma=2}(\text{depth\_to\_base\_surf})(x_{+,m}) - z_{\sigma=2}(\text{depth\_to\_base\_surf})(x_{-,m})\rvert}$.
8. **`xvar_strain_textured_conduit`:** Active transtensional strain-partitioned texture conduit: `xvar_mag_grav_concordance` $\times \bigl(0.5 + 0.5\,\mathrm{expit}(z_{\text{rob}}(\text{geod\_shearrate}))\bigr) \times \bigl(0.5 + 0.5\,\mathrm{expit}(z_{\text{rob}}(\text{geod\_dilaterate}))\bigr)$.
