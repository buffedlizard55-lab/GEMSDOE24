## What H33-B2's 0.2778 likely is and why we can test higher

See `knowledge/14_h33_analysis_2026-10-25.md`. H33-B2's dots are exactly base-minus-catalogue-flank (37,654 vs 40,199 vs 44,090). With DTI = TP/(0.2(TP+FP)+0.8N), removing off-catalogue dots saves α·f FP mass per dot but may lose t TP credit if a dot was nearest to hidden truth within 300 m. Whether pruning helps depends on unknown hidden TP distribution: it improves DTI only if `t·(0.2·F+0.8·N) < 0.2·T·f` (derived in 14_h33_analysis). Catalogue-distance pruning alone cannot guarantee hidden-set gain; sparser is not universally better. Our SGMC lane is *not* pruning — it adds an independent fault proximity signal that is orthogonal to catalogue distance, so it can improve both TP and FP terms where hidden faults coincide with SGMC. This is testable on the holdout, not assumed to beat any organizer score.
