### Root-Cause Discovery: The In-Sample `200–900 m` Dead-Zone Cannibalization Trap

1. **Why `GVC1` (`0.0859` live) and `H66-MGD` (`0.0742` live) Collapsed Despite High Holdout DTI (`0.14876`):**
   - In `GVC1` (`src/gems/gvc.py:568`) and `RSHG1` (`scripts/run_rshg1_lane.py:292`), negative training samples were restricted to `neg_mask = train & ~visible & (dist_vis >= 9.0)` (`>= 900 m` from visible faults), so the gradient-boosted trees **never saw a single negative example inside `100–900 m` of a known fault**.
   - At `Stage 5` (full-footprint export, `scripts/run_gvc1_pipeline.py:576` and `scripts/run_rshg1_lane.py:950`), instead of emitting from out-of-fold predictions, both pipelines refit an **in-sample model on 100% of `catalogue` (`train_mask=fp, visible_mask=catalogue`)**.
   - Because smooth physical fields (`gvc_paleo_sinter_logd`, `gvc_probe2m_anom_1p5km`, `gvc_geotherm_halo_2p5km`, `iso_grav_anom`, `tmi_rtp`, `depth_to_base_surf`) vary slowly over $1.5\text{–}2.5\text{ km}$, the in-sample trees memorized the exact multi-dimensional feature combinations of all `3,199` training fault segments and predicted $\hat{p}(x) \approx 0.95\text{–}1.00$ across the `200–900 m` apron surrounding every training fault.
   - When the allocator masked only $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2.0\text{ px}$ (`200 m`), those in-sample memorized `200–900 m` halos outranked genuine unmapped far-field faults ($\hat{p} \approx 0.45\text{–}0.70$), **dumping `70.4%` (`26,529 / 37,654`) of `GVC1`'s dots into `200–900 m` aprons around already-mapped catalogue faults** and starving the `> 2 km` far-field (`8.5%` of dots vs. `55.1%` of the footprint).
2. **Why `SegmentHoldout` (`Stage 4`) Was Blind to the In-Sample Apron Collapse:**
   - In `SegmentHoldout(buffer_px=15)`, whole connected fault components touching a test quadrant or its `1.5 km` (`15 px`) buffer are hidden (`fold["visible"] = False`). Consequently, inside every test quadrant `fold["valid"]`, the minimum distance to any visible training fault is **$\min(d_{\text{vis}}) = 16.00\text{ px}$ (`1.6 km`)**. `SegmentHoldout` therefore evaluates models exclusively in the $d_{\text{vis}} \ge 16\text{ px}$ far-field (where `GVC1`'s INGENIOUS hydrothermal + tilt + lineament features genuinely excel at `0.14876`), never exposing the $2 < d_{\text{vis}} \le 9\text{ px}$ near-catalogue dead zone that appears only at full-footprint deployment.
3. **Why the Exact Same Bug Caused `GVC1` (`76.82%`) and `RSHG1` (`72.69%`) to Fail the `<= 70%` Competing-Method Overlap Gate:**
   - Across the footprint, `registry-041` (`10GEMSDOE` `gems10-h25-ctx-ridge`, `161,366` px) covers `68.7%` of all pixels at $2 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 5\text{ px}$ within its 3-px halo, vs. only `9.3%` at $d_{\text{cat}} > 20\text{ px}$.
   - For `GVC1`'s `26,529` dots in $2 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 9\text{ px}$, 3-px overlap with `registry-041` is **`82.6%`**, whereas for `GVC1`'s `11,125` dots at $d_{\text{cat}} > 9\text{ px}$, 3-px overlap with `registry-041` is **`63.0%` (`<= 70%` PASS)**.
   - By contrast, `GEMSDOE27`'s `H29-CELF` (`0.2190` live) trained with negatives across all non-catalogue pixels and emitted its full-footprint GeoTIFF from **Dual-Partition Stitched Out-of-Fold predictions (`0.5 * Quadrant_OOF + 0.5 * Rotated45_Sector_OOF`)**, placing **44.3%** of its dots at $d_{\text{cat}} > 20\text{ px}$ (`med = 16.16 px`).

## 3. Locked Experimental Protocol for `H24-DPRF1` (`scripts/run_dprf1_lane.py`)

### 3.1 Three Locked Experimental Arms (Within the 3-Experiment Budget Cap)
All three arms are evaluated on the exact same 4-quadrant `SegmentHoldout(fp, catalogue, buffer_px=15)` (`gems-segment-pooled-v1`, `60,988` withheld positive pixels, `1,000` paired 10 km block-bootstrap draws, `SEED = 20261026`):
1. **`Arm 1: H24-DPRF1` (Locked Primary Candidate)**:
   - **52-channel feature stack**: 30 prepared scale-separated base features + 18 `GVC1` INGENIOUS hydrothermal/tilt/lineament features + 4 `SAHG1` strike-projected conduit features (`G_sat`, `R_alt`, `B_step`, `H_conduit`).
   - **Selective Non-Topographic Nuisance Residualization**: Fit `NuisanceResidualizer(distance_columns=2, alpha=100.0)` strictly on `35,000` uniform pixels from `fold["train"]` and residualize **only** the 9 regional/survey-confounded channels (`tmi_rtp`, `iso_grav_anom`, `depth_to_base_surf`, `cond_surf`, `thk`, `uth`, `uk`, `r_alt`, `b_step`), leaving local scale-separated topographic/lineament/hydrothermal residuals untouched.
   - **Stratified Near-and-Far Negative Sampling ($d_{\text{vis}} \ge 3.0\text{ px}$)**: Sample up to `25,000` positives (`fold["train"] & fold["visible"]`) and `75,000` negatives (`fold["train"] & ~fold["visible"] & (dist_vis >= 3.0)`), stratified `35%` from near-fault piedmont background ($3.0 \le d_{\text{vis}} < 10.0\text{ px}$) and `65%` from far-field background ($d_{\text{vis}} \ge 10.0\text{ px}$). Fit a 2-seed `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` ensemble (`max_iter=190`, `learning_rate=0.055`, `max_leaf_nodes=31`, `min_samples_leaf=80`, `l2_regularization=2.5`).
   - **Relief-Conditioned LiDAR-Gap Calibration + Out-of-Fold Ridge-Coherence Sharpening**: Match tail quantiles across `(block_1..4 × has_lidar × relief_tier)` using `fold["train"] & ~fold["visible"]` reference quantiles, then sharpen 1D fault traces via $p_{\text{sharp}} = 0.72 \, p_{\text{cal}} + 0.28 \, p_{\text{cal}} \cdot \text{clip}((R_{\max}(p_{\text{cal}}) - \bar{R}(p_{\text{cal}})) / P_{99}, 0, 1.5)$ computed strictly inside the target mask.
   - **Two-Regime Submodular 300 m Allocation (`submodular_dti_allocate`, `budget = 37,654` footprint-scaled, `min_spacing_px = 2.8`, `catalogue_clearance_px = 2.0`)**:
     - Regime A (`68%` of fold/footprint budget): Primary ridge-sharpened multi-physics score $p_{\text{sharp}}$.
     - Regime B (`32%` of fold/footprint budget): Concealed Intrabasin, Step-Over & Hydrothermal-Conduit score $p_{\text{conduit}} = p_{\text{sharp}} \cdot (0.30 + 0.70 \, H_{\text{upflow/conduit}}) \cdot (0.30 + 0.70 \, B_{\text{subsurface}}) \cdot (1.0 - 0.50 \, S_{\text{steep-range-front}})$ on remaining eligible pixels ($\ge 2.8\text{ px}$ from Regime A dots and $> 2.0\text{ px}$ from visible catalogue).
2. **`Arm 2: DPRF1-SingleRegime` (Allocator Ablation)**:
   - Identical 52-channel selective-residualized model, stratified $d_{\text{vis}} \ge 3.0\text{ px}$ negative sampling, relief-conditioned calibration, and ridge sharpening, but allocating `100%` of the `37,654`-dot budget via single-regime `submodular_dti_allocate` on $p_{\text{sharp}}$ (testing the exact novelty and holdout impact of the Two-Regime buried-conduit allocation).
3. **`Arm 3: DPRF1-DeadZone9` (Negative-Sampling Ablation)**:
   - Identical 52-channel architecture and Two-Regime allocator, but trained with the legacy `dist_vis >= 9.0` negative dead zone instead of stratified `dist_vis >= 3.0`, directly measuring how eliminating the `3–9 px` dead zone reduces near-catalogue apron cannibalization.

### 3.2 Full-Footprint Inference via Dual-Partition Stitched-OOF + Apron Quantile Debiasing
To guarantee that **zero pixels in the exported competition GeoTIFF are ever predicted in-sample on `catalogue`**:
- Partition 1 (`Quadrant_OOF`): Standard 4-quadrant `SegmentHoldout(fp, catalogue, buffer_px=15)` out-of-fold predictions (`oof_quad`).
- Partition 2 (`Rotated45_Diamond_OOF`): 4-sector $45^\circ$-rotated (`NE-SW` vs. `NW-SE` diagonal split around footprint centroid) whole-component `SegmentHoldout` with the same `15 px` (`1.5 km`) buffer (`oof_rot45`).
- Combined full-footprint out-of-fold surface: $p_{\text{oof}} = 0.50 \, p_{\text{oof,quad}} + 0.50 \, p_{\text{oof,rot45}}$.
- **Visible-Catalogue Apron Quantile Debiasing**: On `fp & ~catalogue`, map the quantile distribution of $p_{\text{oof}}$ inside the near-catalogue belt ($2.0 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 9.0\text{ px}$) onto the out-of-fold far-field reference distribution ($d_{\text{cat}} > 12.0\text{ px}$) so near-catalogue pixels cannot monopolize the top-budget tail solely from regional proximity to known faults.
