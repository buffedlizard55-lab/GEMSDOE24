### New Structural-Validation Insight: Why `SegmentHoldout` vs. Live Scoring Treat Visible Flanks Differently

### 4.3 `NaN`-Preserving Training-Only Nuisance Residualization & Fold-Local Ensemble
- **Regional scalar subset (`REGIONAL_COLS`, 11 cols):** `rtp`, `iso_grav_anom`, `det_elev`, `cond_surf`, `depth_to_base_surf`, `geod_dilaterate`, `geod_shearrate`, `mag_anom`, `tmi`, `geod_2ndinv`, `tc`.
- **Local derivative/scarp subset (`LOCAL_COLS`, 31 cols):** remaining `31` gradient, curvature, tilt-zero, alteration, arc, and LiDAR scarp channels.
- **Strict training-only fit:** On each fold `f` (using `train = fold["train"]` and `visible = fold["visible"]`; and on full footprint using `fp` and `catalogue`), sample up to `30,000` uniform training background indices `bg_sel` (`seed = 20261025 + 100*f`). Fit `gems.residualize.NuisanceResidualizer(alpha=100.0)` on `(C[bg_sel], X[bg_sel])` where `C = [road_m, claim_m, block_1..4]`. Transform `REGIONAL_COLS` with `remove=True` and `LOCAL_COLS` with `remove=False`, **explicitly restoring `np.nan` wherever raw `X` was `NaN`** so unobserved sensor cells remain `np.nan`.
- **Fold-local supervised learner:** Sample `n_pos = min(30000, |train & visible|)` positive pixels and `n_neg = min(90000, |train & ~visible & (dist_visible >= 9)|)` negative pixels. Fit a **2-seed average** (`seeds = [20261025 + 10*f, 20261025 + 10*f + 1]`) of `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=140, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=80, learning_rate=0.06, l2_regularization=8.0, early_stopping=False)`.
- **Training-only regime & nuisance log-odds calibration:** Let $\ell(x) = \text{logit}(\text{clip}(p(x), 10^{-5}, 1 - 10^{-5}))$. On `bg_sel` (training pixels only!):
  1. Standardize $\ell$ separately within `has_lidar` (`np.isfinite(G_sat)`) and `~has_lidar` using the training-subset median and `(p99 - p50)` spread so LiDAR-gap basins have matched upper-tail scale without touching validation pixels.
  2. Fit `Ridge(alpha=100.0)` predicting that standardized log-odds from `NuisanceResidualizer._basis(C[bg_sel])` on `bg_sel`, subtract `(pred_nuisance - mean_train_nuisance)`, and map back via $p_{\text{cal}}(x) = \sigma(\ell_{\text{cal}}(x)) \in (0, 1)$.

### 4.4 Kernel-Expected Trace Scoring & Visible-Halo Clearance
Given calibrated probability map `p_cal`, active domain `domain_mask`, and visible fault mask `visible_mask` (`fold["visible"]` in holdout; `catalogue` at full-footprint inference):
1. Zero out exact visible fault pixels: $p_0 = p_{\text{cal}} \cdot \mathbb{1}[\neg \text{visible\_mask} \land \text{domain\_mask}]$.
2. Convolve $p_0$ with the competition's exact $7\times 7$ discrete 300 m triangular kernel $K(\Delta y, \Delta x) = \max(0, 1 - \sqrt{\Delta y^2 + \Delta x^2}/3)$ and form the ridge-preserving trace-integrated score:
   $$S(u) = \sqrt{p_0(u) \cdot (K * p_0)(u)}.$$
3. Define eligible loci with `200 m` visible-only flank clearance: `eligible = domain_mask & (distance_transform_edt(~visible_mask) > 2.0)`.
4. Emit spaced detections via `gems.emission.priority_poisson(S, eligible, round(0.0073 * domain_mask.sum()), min_dist=3)` (`37,722` full-footprint budget).
