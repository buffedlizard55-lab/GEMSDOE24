## Fixed experiment spec (Rank 1, no tuning)

1. **Inputs:** `footprint` from `data/bridge/sample_submission.tif` (finite mask), `labels` from `data/bridge/labels.tif`, `features.npy` (30 channels, SHA a4ca25b2...), LiDAR U8, Rad U8, Ext U8, geophys bands unpacked from prepared. Verify SHA256 before use.

2. **SAHG1 engineered surfaces:** Call `gems.sahg1.build_surfaces(lidar_bands, rad_bands, ext_bands, geophys_bands, footprint)` → `G_sat`, `R_alt`, `B_step`, `H_conduit`, `support` with 9×9 receptive-field erosion and `verify_receptive_field_support`.

3. **Feature augmentation:** Stack `X_aug = [X_base27 | G_sat, R_alt, B_step, H_conduit]` (31 channels) on footprint; `G_sat,R_alt...` are 0 outside `support` (validly computed, not NaN).

4. **Holdout:** `holdout.SegmentHoldout(footprint, labels, buffer_px=15)`, 4 folds NW/NE/SW/SE, whole 8-connected components hidden globally, 1.5 km collar, visible faults masked pixel-exactly, pooled DTI. CI via `spatial_bootstrap_ci` 10 km blocks, 1,999 draws, seed 20261025.

5. **Leakage canaries:** `context.canary({k: v for each raw band and each of 4 engineered + candidate})` threshold 0.90/0.10 both polarities, 15k per class. Halt if flagged.

6. **Prospective novelty:** `novelty.compare_prospective_registry(surface, fp, priority_rasters+peer_rasters, dots)` pre-placement and final dots, 200k rank sample, radius 3 px. `rho>0.90` or `overlap>0.70` on **method lanes** = duplicate → log and stop (STOP). Calibration controls reported separately; not deleted.

7. **Training:** Per fold, `dist_visible = distance_transform_edt(~fold["visible"])`, `pos = train & visible`, `neg = train & ~visible & (dist_visible≥9 px)`, sample 20k pos / 60k neg (rng seed SEED+fold_id), `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=80, max_leaf_nodes=15, lr=0.08, l2=5.0)`. Predict_proba on fold["test"] support; OOF surface assembled. Also train geomorphic ablation (lidar-only) as control if time permits, else compare to recorded OOF-HGB-D3 0.13000.

8. **Final emission:** Train full-footprint model on all visible-known-masked data (same sampling), predict `p_full`, `eligible = support & ~catalogue`, `final = emission.priority_poisson(p_full, eligible, round(0.0073*fp.sum()), min_dist=3)`. Content ID `submission.scored_content_id(final, fp, catalogue)`, name `gems24-fuse-sahg1-hgb-20261025-{cid}-zeros.tif` (nan twin also).

9. **Writer & validator:** `submission.write_submission(final, template, outside="zero"/"nan")`, `submission.check_variants` both; require `format_valid`, finite [0,1] inside, CRS/shape/transform match. Sanitize via `clip(0,1)` before writer (HGB probas already [0,1]).

10. **Run card:** JSON with hypothesis, mechanism, non-fault mimic, holdout DTI+CI, correlation/overlap vs registry, raster sha256, validator output, submission name + note ≤140 chars, verdict promote/negative. Separate `method_lane_passed` and `literal_all_entries_passed`.
