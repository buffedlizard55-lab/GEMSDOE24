## What the evidence supports about GEMSDOE32 H33-B2

`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` is reported by the owner as **0.2778 (OWNER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED)**; no submission-page receipt is present. Open PR [#61](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24/pull/61) separately addresses the B2 evidence but is still **OPEN / DIRTY**; its analysis is not merged or independently replayed here. The preserved pixel-forensics record says it is a deletion-only subset of the D2.8 emission: the recorded parent has 44,090 positive cells, B2 has 37,654, 6,436 cells were removed within 200 m of the public catalogue, and no cells were added. These are **ARTIFACT-GEOMETRY** facts from the frozen receipt, not hidden-label results. See [receipt](../evidence/priority_review_20261025.json), [metric explanation](metric-integrity-20261026.md) and [archive inspection](../evidence/h33_archive_inspection_c6ea4bb7.json).

The repository's metric implementation defines `D = T / (T + 0.2F + 0.8(G − T))` (up to a small numerical epsilon): `T` is distance-weighted recovered credit, `F` is weighted false-positive mass and `G` is withheld truth mass. Removing dots can improve DTI only if the removed false-positive mass outweighs the lost true credit. For removal with true-credit loss `t` and false-positive-mass reduction `f`, the break-even condition is `t × (0.2F + 0.8G) < 0.2T × f`.

Thus the most defensible *hypothesis* for the reported improvement is more efficient emission / less false-positive mass—not newly discovered faults. It is not possible to infer the deleted cells' hidden true credit from their distance to a public catalogue. The hidden labels and the organizer's TP/FP/FN components are unavailable, and the score claim itself lacks a receipt. Stronger claims that the deleted collar is “always false positive” or that pruning must raise DTI are unsupported. Beating the reported 0.2778 may be possible, but this evidence does not support a numeric probability or leaderboard-score estimate.
