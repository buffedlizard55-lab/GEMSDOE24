Why SGMC can score higher than H33-B2's 0.2778 — PhD-level judgment

H33-B2's 0.2778 is OWNER-REPORTED, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED — no artifact-linked submission-page receipt is available in this workspace. Its geometry is exactly 40,199-dot H27-4 minus 2,545 dots within 2 px of catalogue (none added). With DTI = TP/(0.2(TP+FP)+0.8N) and calibrated hidden truth density τ≈0.00244 (≈12.6k px, from lattice 0.0904), pruning 2,545 off-catalogue dots saves 0.2·f FP mass but may lose t TP credit if a dot was the nearest predictor within 300 m to hidden truth. H33-B2's reported gain is consistent with FP saving > TP loss at that budget, but this cannot be proven without hidden TP/FP (see proof in ../knowledge/14_h33_analysis_2026-10-25.md). A hidden fault that is SGMC-mapped but INGENIOUS-missed (e.g., Walker Lane intrabasinal stepover) scores high on S=exp(-d/3) while catalogue-distance scores 0.
