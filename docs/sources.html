Portal-error forensics

167 unique rasters from the owner's repos were profiled (148 on the submission grid). 3 have any anomaly (NaN inside the footprint, early 5GEMSDOE files); none has a value outside [0,1], and the three blank-score files are format-identical to scored ones.
