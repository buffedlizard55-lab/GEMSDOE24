Next work — completed and remaining

  1. Completed: Prospective registry-overlap separation

    gems.novelty.compare_prospective_registry separates synthetic calibration controls (registry-039 spacing-5 lattice, registry-044 zero placeholder) from the competing method lanes and 83 peer blobs while keeping literal all-entry flags on record.

  2. Completed: Reconstructed same-protocol OOF multi-physics baseline

    oof_multiphysics_hgb_d3 refits a fold-local 27-channel HGB detector strictly on fold["train"] of SegmentHoldout, establishing a leakage-free OOF benchmark of HOLDOUT-DTI 0.13000 (95% CI [0.11565, 0.14396]).

  3. Completed: Strike-projected hydrothermal-geomorphic operator & exact-file C2ST

    H24-SAHG1 achieves HOLDOUT-DTI 0.09672 ([0.08657, 0.10703]) on gems-segment-pooled-v1 and 0.09655 ([0.08270, 0.10976]) on segment-hide-recover-pooled-v1, beating MTR1, ASPC, and its own geomorphic ablation G_0 (+0.01012), and is audited by gems.c2s2 (AUC 0.5826 vs. labels 0.4912).

  4. Completed: Fold-local SAHG1 × OOF HGB fusion & training-only nuisance residualization (H24-FUSE & H24-RSHG1)

    H24-FUSE-HCW integrates G_sat, R_alt, B_step, and H_conduit into a fold-local 31-channel HGB model with training-only NuisanceResidualizer on roads, claims, and acquisition blocks, reducing exact-file C2ST AUC from 0.5826 to 0.5108 (0.4928 on FUSE-RESID, Holm p = 0.870), passing prospective method-lane novelty across all 49 competing method rasters (max 3-px overlap 0.6315 ≤ 0.70 vs. 0.7399 FAIL on unresidualized FUSE-RAW), and reaching HOLDOUT-DTI 0.12372 ([0.11229, 0.13496]). Parallel 42-channel H24-RSHG1 unlocks the 31.8% LiDAR gap (NE_LidarGapHeavy DTI 0.17285), passes C2ST (AUC 0.5088), and reaches 0.12899 primary (0.13137 calibrated pointwise).
