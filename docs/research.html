H24-EUL1 (2026-10-26) — preregistered method and result

Hypothesis: Windowed Euler-deconvolution (structural index N=0, vertical contact) solution clusters in GeoDAWN RTP magnetic and isostatic gravity fields trace basement faults buried under alluvium that scalar tilt/gradient fields and surface mapping miss. Seven label-free Euler channels (solution density, depth and quality for RTP and gravity, gravity x magnetic cross-gradient co-alignment, and the lineament response of the RTP solution density) added to the 52-channel DPRF1 stitched-OOF stack — and gating its concealed Regime-B allocation — recover held-out whole fault segments better than the identical pipeline without them.

Mechanism: Euler homogeneity (Thompson 1982; Reid et al. 1990): in each 7x7 px window the least-squares system x0*Fx + y0*Fy + z0*Fz = u*Fx + v*Fy (N=0) localises the buried contact edge and its depth from the joint geometry of all three field derivatives; accepted tight solutions (depth 50-1500 m, in-window, relative residual < 0.15) accumulate along faulted basement contacts, while shallow volcanic/sedimentary float yields scattered poor-residual solutions. Supervised stitched-OOF, stratified near+far negatives, selective residualization, relief calibration, ridge sharpening, apron debiasing and the two-regime 300 m submodular allocator are reused unchanged from DPRF1, isolating the Euler increment.

Named non-fault mimic: Unfaulted Mesozoic pluton-hornfels intrusive contacts and detrital-magnetite paleochannels, which also produce tight N~0 Euler solutions and aligned gravity/magnetic gradients without any Quaternary faulting or geothermal upflow.

Euler census: RTP 47,322 accepted (0.92% of valid windows, median depth 4.77 px, 100.0% below plane); gravity 5,601 accepted (median 11.0 px). Survey *_vg bands were replaced by self-consistent FFT vertical derivatives after label-free forensics (Amendment A1, knowledge/23_EUL1_prereg_amendment_2026-10-26.md): tmi_vg = ∂(tmi)×0.00990/px (corr 0.9965); iso_grav_anom_vg vs ∂(iso_grav_anom) corr -0.1869.
