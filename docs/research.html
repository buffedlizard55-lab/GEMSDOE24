ASPC physical hypothesis

Decoded uphill-facing scarp fraction × balanced crest/toe curvature × a channel-bank veto × step response × square-root coherence. All factors come from cached, quantized USGS 3DEP-derived descriptors. No mapped fault location or scored raster enters the ranking. A rotational landslide headscarp or terrace riser can mimic it. The 100 m maxima only co-occur: they do not prove a resolved crest/toe pair.

Every input and intermediate factor was screened independently for unusually strong holdout AUC; see the full experiment. The raster is an uncalibrated ranking emitted as binary dots, not a calibrated geothermal probability.

Why a better result is possible, but not promised

For DTI=T/(T+αF+β(N−T)), pruning loses weighted recall L and removes FP penalty M. It helps only if αTM > L(αF+βN). Catalogue proximity alone does not prove L=0: adjacent hidden fault geometry may be real. New fault evidence must improve held-out precision/recall, not merely reproduce or rename the successful dot family. This lane's fixed hypothesis was tested once; negative evidence is not discarded or tuned into a win.
